    Rules come from salary_rules_2026.json. Each country uses its capital city (UK: England). Germany assumes tax class IV with no church tax, France a cadre. Youth reliefs are not applied. Simplified model: no meal vouchers, voluntary or sector pension schemes or company-specific accident rates, and no official rounding. Exchange rates: ECB reference rates, 20 Oct 2026.
